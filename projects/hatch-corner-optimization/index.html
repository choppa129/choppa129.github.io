SJTU Undergraduate Research Program · 2022–2023

Container-Ship Hatch-Corner Structural Optimization

Parametric finite-element modeling, global-to-local submodeling, and multi-island genetic optimization for a container-ship hatch-corner structure.

534 MPaInitial max von Mises stress
424 MPaOptimized max stress
20.6%Simulated stress reduction

Research approach

The project used Patran PCL to automate parameter changes and FE model updates, Nastran for structural analysis, submodeling to focus computation on the hatch-corner region, and Isight's multi-island genetic algorithm to search for improved elliptical geometry.

Optimization result

The reported optimized ellipse parameters were a = 1,386 mm and b = 1,354 mm. Results refer to the specific simulated case described in the PRP report, not physical validation.

Although the report uses the broader term “topology optimization,” the demonstrated case is more precisely elliptical hatch-corner shape optimization.
