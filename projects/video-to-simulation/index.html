01 / OVERVIEW

The challenge

Robotic learning from human videos needs more than visual imitation: a system must recover the geometry, object interactions, and physical structure of a scene so that robot behaviors can be evaluated in simulation.

In the STAR Lab, I work on real-to-sim scene reconstruction as part of a broader video-to-sim-to-real research direction. The goal is to convert observations of human activities into structured, simulation-ready scenes that can support robot-policy learning and evaluation. Deployment to real robots is a research objective, not an outcome claimed here.

02 / MY WORK
