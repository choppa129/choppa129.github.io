01 / OVERVIEW

The challenge

Human hands are remarkably capable at manipulating small, delicate objects. Transferring that dexterity to robots requires precise motion data, robust perception, and careful interaction with the physical world.

In the LIDAR Lab, I contribute to research on robot manipulation for clinical laboratory workflows. My work connects multi-camera calibration, motion capture, gloved-hand data collection, and trajectory processing to support learning and evaluation of manipulation behaviors. This is ongoing research; the site does not claim a completed end-to-end autonomous system.

02 / MY WORK

What I contributed

  • ↗Work on multi-camera and motion-capture calibration for synchronized hand-motion data collection.
  • ↗Process raw recordings into more stable marker and hand-pose trajectories for downstream learning.
  • ↗Explore 3D hand appearance reconstruction and segmentation tools to help interpret hand-object interactions.
  • ↗Support simulation and evaluation workflows for manipulation subtasks involving laboratory objects.
