Radar–Camera Fusion for Field-Robot Perception.

Combining a monocular camera and 77 GHz mmWave radar to enrich object detection for field-robot operation.

01 / OVERVIEW

The challenge

Field robots need perception that remains useful amid visually complex outdoor scenes. Cameras provide rich appearance cues, while mmWave radar contributes range and motion measurements unavailable from an image alone.

I helped build a sensor-fusion pipeline for robots operating in agricultural field environments. The software combines YOLOv8 visual detections with radar measurements, projects radar observations into camera coordinates, and associates radar points with detected targets.

02 / MY WORK

What I contributed

  • ↗Built a YOLOv8-based visual detection pipeline and integrated it with radar measurements.
  • ↗Worked on camera calibration and projection from radar coordinates into the image plane.
  • ↗Implemented nearest-neighbor association between projected radar observations and visual targets.
  • ↗Developed a usable visualization workflow for inspecting detections and radar data together.
